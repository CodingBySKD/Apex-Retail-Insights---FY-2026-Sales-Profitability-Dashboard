# Apex Retail Insights - FY 2026 Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to track retail sales performance, gross profit margins, regional distribution, and channel-wise revenue.

##  Key Features
- **Executive KPIs:** Tracks Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Gross Profit Margin (%), Total Units Sold, Total Orders, and COGS.
- **Trend Analysis:** Monthly Revenue vs. Gross Profit line chart to identify seasonal peaks (July & November).
- **Geographic Insights:** Top 7 Countries by Revenue bar chart and a Donut chart comparing European regions (Central, Northern, Southern, Western).
- **Channel Breakdown:** Pie chart illustrating revenue share across Online Store, Marketplace, Retail Partners, and Corporate channels.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dynamically by **Product Category** (Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, Travel), **Region**, and **Quarter** (Q1–Q4).

##  Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Slicers, Custom Formatting, Charts)
